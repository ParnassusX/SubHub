# SubHub - Comprehensive Project Summary

## 🚀 Project Overview

**SubHub** is a modern, production-ready subscription management application designed to help individuals and businesses track, manage, and optimize their recurring subscriptions. Built with cutting-edge web technologies, SubHub provides a comprehensive solution for subscription oversight with real-time analytics, renewal tracking, and intelligent categorization.

## 🎯 Target Audience

### Primary Users
- **Individual Consumers**: People managing multiple personal subscriptions (streaming services, software, memberships)
- **Small Business Owners**: Entrepreneurs tracking business-related subscriptions and SaaS tools
- **Freelancers & Contractors**: Independent professionals managing various service subscriptions
- **Budget-Conscious Users**: Individuals seeking to optimize subscription spending and eliminate waste

### Secondary Users
- **Financial Advisors**: Professionals helping clients manage recurring expenses
- **Family Managers**: Parents tracking family subscription services
- **Students**: Young adults managing educational and entertainment subscriptions
